About Me

I am a theoretical physicist working as a research associate at IOP Bhubaneswar, India. Other than my research work, I am a teaching assistant. I have gained one year of valuable research experience since completing my Ph.D. degree. My main research focus is on the study of strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions, with a special emphasis on its relevance to neutron/hybrid stars. I am very much interested to expand my research area towards kilonovae, gravitational waves, merging of NSs, and dark matter trapped inside NSs. I have developed all the codes used in my research by myself from scratch to perform numerics.

Research Interests

The non-radial oscillation modes in neutron stars are very much sensitive and directly related to the matter inside it. As if there is a quark-hadron phase transition, the frequency of the mode enhances. So there might be a possibility of a correlation between the non-radial oscillation modes and the neutron star matter properties, like symmetric matter properties, asymmetric matter properties, mass, radius, tidal deformability of the neutron star, etc. I would like to study the correlations of non-radial oscillation frequencies with neutron star matter properties, mass, radius, and tidal deformability of neutron stars. Topics of research (in brief):

Compact Stars

Study of neutron stars, hybrid stars, and protoneutron star evolution using theoretical and computational methods.

Dense Matter Physics

Equation of state of dense nuclear matter, CP violation effects, and the role of QCD interactions under extreme conditions.

Astrophysical Connections

Connecting microscopic properties with observations: gravitational waves, supernovae, and compact object measurements.

Non-Radial Oscillations

Analyzing oscillation modes, f-mode frequencies, and asteroseismology signatures to constrain dense matter properties.
