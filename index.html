About Me

I am a theoretical physicist working as a research associate at the Institute of Physics, Bhubaneswar. My research focuses on dense nuclear and quark matter under extreme conditions and their astrophysical implications, especially neutron stars, hybrid stars, oscillation modes, and gravitational-wave observables.
