About

I am a nuclear astrophysicist studying the properties of strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions in compact stars such as neutron stars and hybrid stars. My work focuses on modeling the equation of state, transport properties, and phase structure of dense QCD matter, and connecting microphysical models to observational data from gravitational waves, pulsars, and heavy-ion collisions.
