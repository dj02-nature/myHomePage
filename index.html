About Me

I am a nuclear astrophysicist working on the behaviour of strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions in compact astrophysical objects such as neutron stars and hybrid stars. My research explores dense nuclear matter, QCD thermodynamics, transport properties, and connections to gravitational-wave observations.

Research Interests

  • Equation of State (EOS) of dense nuclear and quark matter
  • Non-radial oscillations and pulsations of neutron stars
  • Transport coefficients in hot and dense QCD matter
  • Hybrid star structure and stability analysis
  • Gravitational-wave constraints on dense matter physics
