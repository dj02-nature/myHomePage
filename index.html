About Me

I am a nuclear astrophysicist working on the physics of dense matter in compact objects such as neutron stars and hybrid stars. My research explores the phases of strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions, transport properties, and connections with gravitational-wave observations.

I am passionate about theoretical modeling, simulations, and contributing to the understanding of nuclear matter at extreme densities.

Research Areas

🪐

Compact Stars & Dense Matter

Study of neutron stars, hybrid stars, and the equation of state of nuclear matter at high densities.

⚛️

Heavy-Ion Collisions

Exploration of QCD phases under extreme conditions, transport coefficients, and thermal properties of matter.

🌌

Astrophysical Observations

Connections of microphysics with gravitational waves, electromagnetic observations, and multi-messenger astrophysics.
