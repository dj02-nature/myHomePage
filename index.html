About Me

I am a theoretical physicist working as a research associate at IOP Bhubaneswar, India. Other than research, I am a teaching assistant. I have gained one year of research experience since completing my Ph.D. My focus is on strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions, relevant to neutron/hybrid stars. I am interested in expanding to kilonovae, gravitational waves, merging of NSs, and dark matter trapped inside NSs. All my research codes are developed by myself from scratch.

Research Interests

The non-radial oscillation modes in neutron stars are highly sensitive to the matter inside. Quark-hadron phase transitions enhance mode frequencies. I study correlations between non-radial oscillations and neutron star properties: symmetric/asymmetric matter, mass, radius, tidal deformability, etc. Topics of research (in brief):

Compact Stars

Neutron stars, hybrid stars, protoneutron star evolution using theoretical/computational methods.

Dense Matter Physics

Equation of state, CP violation effects, and QCD interactions under extreme conditions.

Astrophysical Connections

Linking microscopic properties to observations: gravitational waves, supernovae, compact object measurements.

Non-Radial Oscillations

Oscillation modes, f-mode frequencies, and asteroseismology for constraining dense matter.
