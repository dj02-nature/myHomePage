About Me

I am a nuclear astrophysicist studying dense QCD matter under extreme conditions in compact stars such as neutron stars and hybrid stars. My research focuses on equation of state modeling, transport properties, and connecting dense-matter microphysics to multi-messenger astrophysics.

Research Interests

  • Equation of state of dense nuclear and quark matter
  • Transport coefficients and thermal evolution of neutron stars
  • Hybrid stars and phase transitions at high density
  • Gravitational-wave constraints on dense matter
  • Connections between heavy-ion collisions and astrophysical matter
