About Me

I am a theoretical physicist working as a research associate at IISER Bhopal. My research focuses on strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions, with a particular emphasis on neutron/hybrid stars and dense QCD matter. I develop my own numerical codes and aim to connect microscopic nuclear physics with macroscopic astrophysical observations.

Future Research Plan

I aim to study the correlations between non-radial oscillation frequencies of neutron stars and their macroscopic properties, including mass, radius, and tidal deformability. My future directions include:

  • Binary Neutron Star Mergers: Hadron–quark transitions and gravitational-wave signatures.
  • Non-Radial Oscillations: Diagnosing neutron star interior composition and exotic phases.
  • AI and Bayesian Frameworks: Constraining neutron star EOS using multi-messenger data.
  • Interdisciplinary Connections: Linking heavy-ion physics with dense star matter.
