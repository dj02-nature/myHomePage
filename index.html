About Me

I am a theoretical physicist working as a research associate at IISER Bhopal. My main research focus is the study of strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions, particularly inside neutron/hybrid stars. I develop numerical codes from scratch and explore connections to kilonovae, gravitational waves, and dark matter in NSs.

Education

PhD (Physics): Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar
MSc (Physics): Banaras Hindu University, Varanasi
BSc (Physics): Banaras Hindu University, Varanasi

Future Research Plan

I am studying non-radial oscillation modes in neutron stars to understand correlations with NS matter properties like mass, radius, and tidal deformability.
