About Me

I am a theoretical physicist working as a research associate at IOP Bhubaneswar, India. Other than my research work, I am a teaching assistant. I have gained one year of valuable research experience since completing my Ph.D. degree. My main research focus is on the study of strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions, with a special emphasis on its relevance to neutron/hybrid stars. I am very much interested to expand my research area towards kilonovae, gravitational waves, merging of NSs, dark matter trapped inside NSs. I have developed all the codes used in my research by myself from scratch to perform numerics.

Research

The non-radial oscillation modes in neutron stars are highly sensitive and directly related to the matter inside them. If a quark–hadron phase transition occurs, the mode frequency increases. Therefore, oscillation frequencies may correlate with neutron star matter properties—symmetric/asymmetric matter behavior, mass, radius, tidal deformability, etc.

  • Dense QCD matter
  • Hybrid stars & quark–hadron transitions
  • Transport properties under extreme conditions
  • Neutron star oscillations and asteroseismology
  • Dark matter admixed neutron stars
