    About

    I am a theoretical physicist working as a research associate at IOP Bhubaneswar, India. My research centres on dense nuclear and quark matter under extreme conditions and their astrophysical implications: neutron stars, hybrid stars, oscillation modes and gravitational-wave signatures. I build and maintain my own numerical codes for simulations and analysis.

    Research Interests

    I investigate how microphysics (equation of state, phase transitions, nuclear saturation properties) affects macroscopic observables of compact objects: mass, radius, tidal deformability and oscillation spectra. Current projects include correlations of f-mode frequencies with dense matter properties, impacts of quark-hadron transitions on modes, and machine-learning assisted EOS inference.

    Compact Stars

    Neutron & hybrid stars, protoneutron star evolution and maximum mass constraints.

    Dense Matter Physics

    Equation of state, QCD effects, axions and CP-violation in dense matter.

    Asteroseismology

    f-modes, non-radial oscillations and gravitational-wave signatures.

    Machine Learning

    ML approaches for EOS inference and universal relations.
