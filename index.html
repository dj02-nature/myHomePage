About Me

I am a theoretical physicist at Institute of Physics, Bhubaneswar, India. My research focuses on strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions, including neutron stars, hybrid stars, and the connection between microphysics and observable astrophysical phenomena. I develop numerical codes for simulations and analysis of dense matter, transport coefficients, and non-radial oscillation modes.

Research Interests

My work aims to explore properties of strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions. I investigate the different phases of dense nuclear and quark matter, their transport coefficients, thermodynamics, and the impact on compact astrophysical objects, including the study of oscillation modes and gravitational-wave signals.

Compact Stars

Neutron stars, hybrid stars, mass-radius relations, stability analysis.

Dense Matter

Equation of state, QCD phases, electrical & thermal transport properties, Seebeck coefficient.

Oscillations & Asteroseismology

Non-radial oscillations, f-modes, gravitational-wave observables, universal relations.

Heavy-Ion Collisions

RHIC/LHC physics, quark-gluon plasma, transport coefficients of hot dense matter.
