Research

The non-radial oscillation modes in neutron stars are sensitive probes of dense matter. A quark–hadron phase transition can significantly alter the oscillation frequencies. I study correlations between these modes and neutron star matter properties, including symmetry energy parameters, tidal deformability, radius, and equation of state.
