About Me

I am a theoretical physicist working as a research associate at IOP Bhubaneswar, India. Other than my research work, I am a teaching assistant. I have gained one year of valuable research experience since completing my Ph.D. My main research focus is the study of strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions, with emphasis on neutron/hybrid stars. I am interested in kilonovae, gravitational waves, NS mergers, and dark-matter admixed compact stars. I develop the numerical codes used in my research from scratch.

Research Interests

The non-radial oscillation modes in neutron stars are sensitive probes of the matter inside. A quark–hadron transition typically modifies mode frequencies. I study correlations between non-radial oscillation frequencies and neutron star matter properties — symmetry energy, mass, radius, tidal deformability — using theoretical modeling, Bayesian inference, and machine learning.
