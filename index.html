About Me

I am a theoretical physicist at IOP Bhubaneswar, India. My research focuses on dense nuclear and quark matter under extreme conditions and their astrophysical implications: neutron stars, hybrid stars, oscillation modes, and gravitational-wave signatures. I build numerical codes for simulations and analysis.

Research Interests

I study how microphysics affects macroscopic observables of compact objects: mass, radius, tidal deformability, and oscillation spectra.

Compact Stars

Neutron & hybrid stars, protoneutron star evolution.

Dense Matter

Equation of state, QCD effects, axions.

Asteroseismology

f-modes, non-radial oscillations, GW signatures.

Machine Learning

ML for EOS inference and universal relations.
