About Me

I am a theoretical physicist working as a research associate at IOP Bhubaneswar, India. Other than my research work, I am a teaching assistant. I have gained one year of valuable research experience since completing my Ph.D. degree. My main research focus is on the study of strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions, with special emphasis on neutron/hybrid stars. I am also interested in kilonovae, gravitational waves, NS mergers, and dark matter inside neutron stars. I develop all numerical codes used in my research from scratch.

Research

The non-radial oscillation modes in neutron stars are highly sensitive to the matter inside them. A quark–hadron phase transition can significantly modify mode frequencies. I study how these oscillations correlate with nuclear matter properties, mass, radius, and tidal deformability of neutron stars.
