About

I am a theoretical physicist working as a research associate at IOP Bhubaneswar, India. My research centres on dense nuclear and quark matter under extreme conditions and their astrophysical implications.

Education

Ph.D. Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (2017 – 2023)
Thesis: Properties of strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions
Defense: 15 May 2023
Supervisor: Prof. Hiranmaya Mishra (hiranmaya@niser.ac.in)

Employment

Post-doctoral fellow Institute of Physics, Bhubaneswar — Oct 2024 – Present

Research Interests

Investigating how microphysics affects macroscopic observables of compact objects: mass, radius, tidal deformability and oscillation spectra.

Compact Stars

Neutron & hybrid stars; protoneutron star evolution.

Dense Matter Physics

EOS, QCD effects, axions and CP-violation in dense matter.
